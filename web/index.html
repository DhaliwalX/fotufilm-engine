fotufilm — Physical Film Development & Simulation

A physically modelled film simulation engine in WebAssembly and Swift. Rather than applying a superficial look or color lookup tables (LUTs), fotufilm models the complete chain of physical processes between scene light and a finished print or transparency.

The Physical Emulsion Pipeline

  1. Exposure: bounded RGB-to-reflectance recovery fitted to 28,665 open measured spectra, with per-layer sensitivities integrated under CIE D65.
  2. Flare: Lens veiling glare scattering scene light uniformly.
  3. Emulsion Diffusion: Light scattering inside the emulsion pack (turbidity / MTF).
  4. Halation: Base reflection, Fresnel reflectance, and angle-dependent bounce exposure.
  5. DIR Couplers & Adjacency: Development inhibitor diffusion between layers and edge Mackie lines.
  6. Development: Silver/dye density via analytic Hurter & Driffield (H&D) characteristic curves.
  7. Grain: Stochastic Poisson clump field scaling physically with format size.
  8. Output Medium: Optical enlarger printing, Beer–Lambert dye absorption, and paper tone reproduction.
